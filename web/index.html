Journey Log

    1Objective

    Become a Dhara Rakshak (Earth Guardian). Travel through the eight realms, solve interdisciplinary challenges, collect Shakti Tokens and complete the Grand Green Challenge at FINISH. The qualifying player with the most Shakti Tokens wins.

    2Players

    2–6 players or teams · age 8+ · 45–60 minutes. Everyone starts on START with 5 Shakti Tokens, no Karma markers and no Emblems. The next player in turn order is the Game Keeper: they read cards aloud and judge open tasks.

    3How to play

    1. Roll the die (click it) and move forward.
    2. Resolve the space you land on — usually a challenge card with 30 seconds to answer.
    3. Correct answer: 2 Shakti Tokens. A practical environmental solution earns +1 bonus (max 3 per ordinary challenge).
    4. A wrong answer earns nothing; it carries a penalty only when the space says so.
    5. Spaces reached by a shortcut, setback or reward move are not activated.
    6. Press Next player to pass the turn.

    4Karma

    If you cannot pay a penalty, surrender what you have and take a Karma marker. Tokens never go below zero. While carrying Karma you cannot attempt a Tapasya or Mahashakti challenge — and you cannot win.

    Clear one marker per turn, before rolling: answer a recovery challenge (no reward; a wrong answer ends your turn), pay 3 tokens, or choose removal on a Divine Grace space.

    5Tapasya Trial

    On a Tapasya Portal, with 3+ tokens and no Karma, you may wager 3 tokens on three stages in 2 minutes. Success: 8 tokens and 3 spaces forward. Failure: the wager is lost, 3 spaces back and one Karma marker.

    6Mahashakti Challenge

    Three rare portals, each usable once. Land exactly, hold 6+ tokens and no Karma, and declare before seeing the card. Wager 4 tokens on five stages — Gyaan, Vigyaan, Vaani, Dharma, Ekagrata — in 3 minutes.

    Success: 12 tokens, 6 spaces forward and a Mahashakti Emblem. Failure: the wager is lost, 4 spaces back and one Karma marker. An Emblem cancels one future movement setback — never a token loss, Karma, or a failed high-risk challenge.

    7Dhara Rules & the Grand Green Challenge

    The first time anyone enters a realm, a waste-management Dhara Rule is revealed. Remember them!

    At FINISH, face seven stages: Mathematics, Science, English, Hindi, Social Science, Memory (recall the Dhara Rules) and a Creative Action. Pass 4 of 5 subjects plus both tasks to qualify and earn 5 bonus tokens. If you fail, stay at FINISH and try again next turn.

    8Winning

    When the first player qualifies, the round is completed so everyone has had an equal number of turns. Outstanding Karma must be cleared before the final count. The qualified player without Karma and with the most Shakti Tokens wins; ties are settled by a sudden-death question.
